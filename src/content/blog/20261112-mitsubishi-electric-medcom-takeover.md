Mitsubishi Electric's agreement to buy the remaining 51% of Polish manufacturer Medcom would bring the Warsaw-based railway equipment supplier under full ownership. The Japanese group first invested in Medcom in 2016.

Medcom supplies electrical equipment for mainline trains and urban rail vehicles. Its work includes propulsion controls, auxiliary power systems and industrial power electronics, making it a strategic partner in efforts to improve transport energy efficiency.

The deal was announced on August 5, 2026. [Railway Gazette International's August 12 report](https://www.railwaygazette.com/news/2026/08/12/mitsubishi-electric-to-take-full-ownership-of-polish-traction-equipment-manufacturer/) said completion was expected in the third quarter, subject to regulatory approval. That was the timetable announced at the time, not confirmation that the acquisition has since closed.

Mitsubishi Electric outlined plans to develop Warsaw as a European hub for research, engineering and manufacturing in power electronics. Silicon-carbide technology is an important part of the partnership.

*This English adaptation follows Mundo Trilhos' coverage published on November 12. The underlying announcement dates from August; the sources reviewed do not establish the transaction's subsequent completion.*
