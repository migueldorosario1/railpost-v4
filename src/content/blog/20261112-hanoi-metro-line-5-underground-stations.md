Construction at two underground stations is a significant element of Hanoi's planned Metro Line 5, a 39.6 km route intended to operate with fully automated trains.

According to the coverage republished by Mundo Trilhos, civil works began on July 24, 2026, at Quần Ngựa, designated S1, and Vành đai 1, designated S3. Both stations belong to the line's underground central section.

These details appear in [Railway Gazette International's urban rail round-up dated August 12](https://www.railwaygazette.com/urban/2026/08/12/urban-rail-industry-news-round-up-17/), the source cited by the Portuguese report. The station works are therefore an earlier project milestone, rather than a new construction announcement this November.

The planned line combines underground infrastructure with automated operations. Building stations is one stage of a much larger delivery process that also requires tracks, trains, power supply, signaling and testing before passengers can use the service.

The development forms part of the wider expansion of rail-based public transport in the Vietnamese capital. The sources reviewed for this edition do not establish a new opening date or an updated construction-completion percentage.
