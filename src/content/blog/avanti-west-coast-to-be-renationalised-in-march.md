The Guardian reports that Avanti West Coast will be renationalised in March.
The operator runs services on the West Coast Main Line.
The move returns the franchise to public ownership under the government.
Avanti West Coast is currently operated by a private company.
The renationalisation is scheduled to take place in March.
The Guardian is the source of this report.
