Railway Age is accepting nominations for the 10th Annual Women in Rail Award, with a submission deadline of Oct. 1.

The award marks its 10th annual edition, according to Railway Age.

Nominations must be submitted by the Oct. 1 deadline set by the outlet.

Railway Age announced the deadline in its coverage of the award.

Additional details on the nomination process are available through Railway Age.
